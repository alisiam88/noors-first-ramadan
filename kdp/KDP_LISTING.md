### Description (paste into the description box)

<b>It's Yahia's very first Ramadan, and there is so much to discover!</b><br><br>
Join little Yahia and his big sister Kayan as the crescent moon appears and the holy month begins. Page by page, children follow their Ramadan from the first moon to a joyful Eid: hanging lanterns and stars, waking up for suhoor, walking to the mosque with Baba, reading the Quran together, sharing food with neighbors, breaking the fast with dates, welcoming Grandma and Grandpa for iftar, and celebrating Eid with the whole family.<br><br>
Each picture comes with a short, gentle story line, so parents can read aloud while little ones color, and children learn what Ramadan means through a story they help bring to life.<br><br>

<li>A "This Book Belongs To" page and a Ramadan Star Certificate to award at the end of the month</li>
<li>Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages</li>
</ul>
<b>A meaningful Ramadan and Eid gift</b> for sons, daughters, grandchildren, nieces and nephews, and a lovely way to start new family traditions during the holy month.
